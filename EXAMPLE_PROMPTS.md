## Tips for Best Results

- **Be specific**: "Research transformer attention mechanisms in vision transformers" beats "explain AI"
- **Ask follow-ups**: The system maintains conversation memory across turns
- **Upload docs first**: Upload relevant PDFs before asking questions for RAG-grounded responses
- **Request reports**: End with "generate a full research report" to trigger the Reporter Agent
